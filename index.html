Fiddle of Transmutation

Tune the fiddle to a material, then roll a concrete 5e-style effect

Homebrew · Not official WotC content
How to use at the table

Search or filter to the material your fiddle is made of / tuned to. Tap Play Fiddle (usually an action). Read the mechanical text; the DM adjudicates stacking and concentration.

  • Each material builds its own effect table from affinity + a signature effect (homebrew).
  • Use your spell save DC if you have one; otherwise the material’s listed DC.
  • Unless noted, range is 30 ft to a creature that can hear the tune.
  • Playing is audible — Stealth has disadvantage while you play.
